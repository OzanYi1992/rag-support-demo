# Technical Support

This page describes how our engineering desk handles incoming faults, which
response times apply, and how a case moves up through the escalation stages.

## Contact channels

Faults are accepted through the customer portal or by telephone. The portal is
available around the clock; the telephone desk operates on business days from
08:00 to 18:00 local time at our warehouse location.

Every fault receives a case number. Please quote it in all further correspondence,
because our desk sorts by case number rather than by sender.

## Severity classes

We sort every incoming fault into one of three classes. The class is assigned by
our desk, not by the reporting party, and it may be revised once further detail
becomes available.

| Class | Meaning | First reply within |
| --- | --- | --- |
| One | Production has stopped, no workaround exists | 4 business hours |
| Two | Reduced function, a workaround exists | 8 business hours |
| Three | Question, documentation request, cosmetic defect | 2 business days |

The first reply is a substantive answer from an engineer, not an automated
acknowledgement of receipt. Business hours are counted only inside the telephone
desk window stated above.

## What a class one case requires

A class one case is only opened when the reporting party confirms that operation
has ceased entirely and that no alternative route exists. We ask for the serial
number, a photograph of the assembly and a short account of what changed before
the failure appeared.

Without a serial number we cannot open a class one case, because the engineering
desk needs the manufacturing batch to look up known deviations.

## Escalation stages

If a class one case is still unresolved after two business days, it moves to the
second stage and a senior engineer takes ownership. After five business days it
moves to the third stage, where the component manufacturer is drawn in and a
replacement part is dispatched in parallel with the investigation.

The reporting party is informed at each transition. No case is closed without an
explicit confirmation from the reporting party that operation has resumed.

## Remote diagnosis

For programmable components we offer a remote session. It is scheduled within one
business day of the request and lasts up to ninety minutes. A session requires an
outbound connection on port 443 and a person on site who can power-cycle the
assembly.

Remote diagnosis is free of charge for cases in class one and class two. For
class three we charge a flat fee of 90 euro net.

## Out of scope

Our desk does not carry out programming work, does not adapt firmware to a
specific installation and does not provide advice on third-party components that
were not purchased from us. Such requests are answered with a referral rather than
a solution.
