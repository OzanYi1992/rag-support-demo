# Gear Care and Repairs

Outdoor gear lasts longer when it is looked after, and most damage is repairable.
This page covers care, our repair service, and what counts as a fault.

## Washing waterproof garments

A waterproof shell stops working long before it leaks. What usually fails first
is the water-repellent finish on the outer face: the fabric wets out, the
membrane cannot breathe, and the garment feels damp from the inside.

Wash a shell at **30 degrees** on a gentle cycle with a technical detergent.
Never use fabric softener; it coats the fabric and destroys the finish for good.
Tumble dry on low for 20 minutes afterwards, or press with a warm iron through a
cloth. Heat is what reactivates the finish.

Reproof when water no longer beads after washing and drying. Wash-in products
suit unlined shells, spray-on products suit garments with an insulated lining.

## Down insulation

Store down loose, never compressed. A down bag left in its stuff sack for a
season loses loft that does not come back.

Wash down at **30 degrees** with a down-specific detergent, rinse twice, and dry
with three clean tennis balls over several low-heat cycles. Expect four hours.
Down that is put away damp will clump and can mildew inside the baffles.

## Boots

Brush off mud while it is still wet. Dried mud draws moisture out of leather.

Dry boots at room temperature, away from radiators and never in direct sun.
Forced heat shrinks leather and can separate a sole from the midsole.

Treat leather boots with a wax or cream every ten days of use. Fabric boots take
a spray proofer instead.

## Tents

Pitch a tent at home to dry it before storage, even if it only felt damp coming
down. A tent packed wet can grow mould in 48 hours, and mould on a coated fly is
permanent.

Never machine wash a tent. Sponge it with cold water and leave it to dry.

## Faults and the two-year period

A fault is a failure of material or workmanship: a seam that opens under normal
use, a zip that parts without damage, delamination away from an impact point.

Faults reported within **two years** of delivery are repaired or replaced at no
charge. Outside that period the repair prices above apply.

Field wear is not a fault. Abrasion, punctures, UV fading and a worn-through
sole are consequences of use, and we repair them at the prices above.
