# Frequently Asked Questions

Short answers to the questions we hear most often. Where another page has the
full rules, the answer names it.

**Do I need an account to order?**
No, you can order as a guest. An account is only needed for Trail Club, and a
guest order can be added to a new account later, as described on the Trail Club
page.

**I forgot my password. How do I get back into my account?**
Use Forgot password on the sign-in page. We email a link that lets you choose a
new password. The password link is valid for two hours.

**Do you deliver outside Sweden?**
Yes, we deliver worldwide. Mainland Denmark, Finland and Norway are Zone B, and
every country beyond them is Zone C. Delivery times and charges for each zone
are on the Where We Ship page.

**Which currency are your prices in?**
Euro, on every page and for every zone, Sweden included. A card in another
currency is converted by the card issuer.

**Do I have to pay import duty?**
Only for parcels that leave the EU, for example to Norway or the United
Kingdom. The destination country charges the duty, and the recipient pays it.

**How do I find my size?**
Measure as described on the Size Guide page and compare your measurements with
its tables. Our footwear uses EU sizes.

**Is an item in stock, and what is its price?**
The price and the stock level of each product are shown on its product page.
They are not listed on these pages. For an item on back order, the product page
shows the expected dispatch week.

**My parcel has an item I did not order, or an item is missing. What now?**
Tell us within 14 days of delivery and add a photo of the delivery note. You
can do this in your account under Orders or by answering the dispatch
confirmation. We send the missing or the correct item at no charge, with
Tracked service. An item sent by mistake comes back on a free return label.

**My parcel was damaged in transit. What should I do?**
Keep the packaging and tell us within 5 working days of delivery, by answering
the dispatch confirmation. Add photos of the parcel and of the damaged item.
You choose between a replacement and a full refund.

**Can I pay in instalments?**
No. We accept card, direct debit from an account in Zone A or Zone B, gift
cards, and invoice for Trail Club members at Summit level.

**Can I get a receipt for my order?**
Yes. The dispatch confirmation includes a receipt as a PDF, and every receipt
stays available in your account under Orders. For a guest order the receipt is
in the dispatch confirmation only. On our pages, invoice means a payment
method, described on the Payment and Refunds page.

**Can I buy a gift card?**
Yes, as a digital gift card from 25 to 500 euro. A gift card is valid for three
years from the day it is bought.

**Can you gift wrap my order?**
No. You can send an order to a delivery address that differs from the billing
address, and the parcel contains a delivery note without prices.

**Can I rent a tent or other gear?**
No. We do not rent out gear, and we do not buy back or resell used gear.

**Can you send gas cartridges abroad?**
No. Gas cartridges and liquid fuel are delivered to Zone A addresses only.

**Do you sell to companies?**
No. Fellgate Outdoor sells to private customers only.

**Do you have a shop I can visit?**
No. Fellgate Outdoor sells by mail order only.
There is no walk-in shop or showroom.
