# Our Range

This page describes what Fellgate Outdoor sells, grouped the way the shop is
organised, and what we do not stock.

## Categories

| Category | What it covers |
| --- | --- |
| Footwear | Leather and fabric hiking boots, approach shoes, gaiters, hiking socks |
| Clothing | Waterproof shells, down and synthetic jackets, fleeces, base layers, trousers, gloves and hats |
| Sleeping | Down and synthetic sleeping bags, sleeping mats, pillows |
| Shelter | Tents for one to four people, tarps, footprints |
| Packs | Daypacks, trekking packs, pack frames, rain covers |
| Cooking | Stoves, gas cartridges, liquid fuel, pots and mugs |
| Ropes and climbing | Rope and webbing sold by the metre, harnesses, helmets, carabiners |
| Avalanche safety | Transceivers, probes and shovels |
| Food and water | Trail meals, snacks, water filters and treatment tablets |
| Gift cards | Digital gift cards from 25 to 500 euro |

Sizes for footwear and clothing are explained on the Size Guide page. Gas
cartridges and liquid fuel have delivery limits, described on the Where We Ship
page.

## Our own label

Most clothing, tents and packs are made for us under the Fellgate label. Boots,
stoves and avalanche equipment come from specialist manufacturers, and the
manufacturer is named on each product page.

## What we do not stock

We do not sell bicycles, kayaks or canoes, fishing equipment, or skis and
snowboards. Winter equipment is limited to avalanche safety.

We do not rent out gear, and we do not buy back or resell used gear.
