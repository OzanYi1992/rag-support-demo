# Trail Club Membership

Trail Club is our free loyalty programme. This page covers the levels, how
points work, and what each level actually changes.

## Joining

Membership is free and opens with an email address. There is no card; the
account is the membership.

An order placed before joining can be added to the account within **30 days** of
dispatch by entering the order number under Orders. Points are then credited as
if the order had been placed as a member. Older orders cannot be added.

## The three levels

The level follows what you spent in the **last 12 months**, counted on a rolling
basis rather than by calendar year.

| Level | Spend in the last 12 months |
| --- | --- |
| Path | from the first order |
| Ridge | 400 and above |
| Summit | 1,200 and above |

A level is reached the moment the threshold is passed and is held for 12 months
from that day. Dropping below the threshold does not remove the level before
those 12 months are up.

## What each level changes

| | Path | Ridge | Summit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Return window | 30 days | 100 days | 100 days |
| Return postage, Zone B and C | 9.90 | 9.90 | free |
| Points per 10 spent | 1 | 1 | 2 |
| Invoice as payment method | no | no | yes |
| Repair turnaround | 14 working days | 14 working days | 7 working days |
| Early access to new season | no | 48 hours | 7 days |

The longer return window is the change members notice most. It applies to the
order that was placed while signed in, not to the account as a whole, so an
order placed as a guest keeps the 30-day window even on a Summit account.

## Points

One point is earned per 10 spent, doubled at Summit level. Points are credited
at dispatch, not at checkout, and are removed again if the order is returned.

Points are not earned on shipping charges, on gift cards, or on items bought in
a clearance event.

100 points are worth 10 against a future order. Points cannot be exchanged for
cash and cannot be moved between accounts.

## Expiry

Points expire **24 months** after the month in which they were earned. The oldest
points are always spent first.

The account shows the next expiry date and the amount due to expire. We send a
reminder 30 days before points lapse, to the email address on the account.

## Closing an account

An account can be closed at any time under Settings. Closing forfeits any points
on it immediately; there is no grace period, and points cannot be restored once
the account is closed.

An open return or repair holds the closure until that case is finished.
