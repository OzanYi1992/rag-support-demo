# Payment and Refunds

This page lists the payment methods we accept, when money is actually taken, and
how long a refund takes to arrive.

## Accepted methods

- Credit and debit card.
- Direct debit from an account in Zone A or Zone B.
- Gift card, alone or combined with one other method.
- Invoice, for Trail Club members at Summit level only.

We do not accept cash on delivery, cheques, or payment in instalments. A basket
cannot be split across two cards.

## When money is taken

Card payments are **authorised** at checkout and **captured** at dispatch. The
authorisation reserves the amount without moving it. On a statement it may look
like a charge; it is released if the order is cancelled.

Direct debit is collected two working days after dispatch.

Invoice is due **21 days** from the invoice date, which is the dispatch date.

Gift card balances are deducted immediately at checkout and are not refunded to
the original card.

## Failed payments

An authorisation can fail for a reason the issuer does not share with us. The
order then stays on Received and we email a link to try another method. The link
is valid for 48 hours; after that the order is released and stock goes back.

A failed direct debit collection is retried once after five working days. A
second failure closes the order and blocks direct debit on the account until it
is settled.

## Refunds

A refund goes back to the method that paid, always. We cannot refund a card
payment to a bank account, or a direct debit to a card.

| Method | Refund reaches you after |
| --- | --- |
| Card | 5 working days from the day we process it |
| Direct debit | 3 working days |
| Gift card | immediately, as a new gift card balance |
| Invoice | the invoice is reduced, or credited if already paid |

We process a refund within **two working days** of the returned parcel reaching
our warehouse and passing inspection. The times in the table start from that
point, not from the day the parcel was sent back.

## Partial refunds

Where only part of an order comes back, the refund covers the returned lines and
their share of any shipping charge. Where the remaining order value falls below
the free shipping threshold, the shipping charge that would have applied is
deducted from the refund.

Return label costs for Zone B and Zone C are deducted as described in
`returns-and-exchanges`.

## Price adjustments

If an item drops in price within **14 days** of your order being dispatched, we
refund the difference on request. This does not apply to items reduced in a
clearance event, where the price is marked as final.
