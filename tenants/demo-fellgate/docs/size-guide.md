# Size Guide

This page explains how our sizes are measured and how to choose between two
sizes. All figures are body measurements in centimetres, not garment
measurements.

## Measuring yourself

Measure in the evening, when feet and body are at their largest, and over the
layer you plan to wear next to the skin.

- **Foot length**: stand on a sheet of paper with your heel against a wall and
  mark the tip of your longest toe. Measure both feet and use the longer one.
- **Chest**: around the fullest part, under the arms, with the tape level.
- **Waist**: around your natural waist, above the hip bones.
- **Inside leg**: from the crotch to the floor, without shoes.

length is within **0.3 cm** of the limit for a size, take the next size up.

## Tops

Jackets, fleeces and base layers share one size scale, measured at the chest.

| Size | Women, chest | Men, chest |
| --- | --- | --- |
| XS | 80 to 83 cm | 86 to 89 cm |
| S | 84 to 87 cm | 90 to 95 cm |
| M | 88 to 93 cm | 96 to 101 cm |
| L | 94 to 99 cm | 102 to 107 cm |
| XL | 100 to 105 cm | 108 to 113 cm |
| XXL | 106 to 111 cm | 114 to 119 cm |

Shells are cut with room for a fleece and a base layer underneath. Take your
usual size, and go one size up only if you wear a down jacket under the shell.
Base layers are cut close to the body and are not meant to be sized up.

76 cm, regular up to 81 cm, and long up to 86 cm.

## Between two sizes

In tops and trousers, let the layer underneath decide: the smaller size for a
close fit over a base layer, the larger one for room over insulation.
