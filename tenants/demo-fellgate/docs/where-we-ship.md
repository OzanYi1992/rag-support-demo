# Where We Ship

This page names the countries in each delivery zone, with Standard delivery
times and shipping charges, the currency of our prices, and what changes for
parcels that leave the EU.

## Our warehouse

Every order ships from our own warehouse in Sweden. Where our other pages say
home country, they mean Sweden, and working days and public holidays follow the
Swedish calendar.

## Countries by zone

| Zone | Where it applies | Standard delivery | Shipping below 60 euro | Shipping from 60 euro |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Zone A | Mainland Sweden | 3 working days | 5.90 | free |
| Zone B | Mainland Denmark, Finland and Norway | 5 working days | 11.90 | 6.90 |
| Zone C | Every other country worldwide, and island addresses without a fixed road link in any country | 9 working days | 24.90 | 19.90 |

Tracked and Next Day services, the dispatch cut-off and the rules for oversized
items are on the Shipping and Delivery page.

Denmark counts as Zone B because road freight reaches it over the Øresund Bridge.
Finland and Norway share a land border with Sweden.

An island joined to the road network by a bridge or a tunnel counts as mainland.
An island reached only by ferry or plane is Zone C, even inside Sweden. Gotland
is the most common example.

Every other country means the rest of Europe, for example Germany and the
United Kingdom, and every country outside Europe,
for example the United States, Canada and Japan. We deliver worldwide.

## Currency

All prices on our pages are in euro, in every zone: shipping charges, repair
prices, return label costs and the Trail Club thresholds. We do not show prices
in Swedish, Danish or Norwegian kronor. A card in another currency is charged in
euro, and any conversion is made by the card issuer.

## Parcels leaving the EU

Sweden, Denmark and Finland are members of the EU; Norway is not. Parcels to
Norway and to every other country outside the EU pass through customs in the
destination country. Import duty and fees charged there are not part of our
prices and are paid by the recipient.

## Items with delivery limits

Gas cartridges and liquid fuel are delivered to Zone A addresses only. Checkout
does not accept them for an address in Zone B or Zone C.
