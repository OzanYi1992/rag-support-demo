# Shipping and Delivery

This page covers where we ship, how long each service takes, and what happens
when a parcel does not arrive on time.

## Delivery zones

We ship to three zones. The zone follows the delivery address, not the billing
address.

- **Zone A** — mainland addresses within our home country.
- **Zone B** — neighbouring countries reachable by road freight.
- **Zone C** — island addresses and everything beyond Zone B.

Orders to Zone C are handed to a partner carrier once they leave our warehouse.
We can see the handover but not the onward route.

## Services and transit times

Transit time is counted in working days and starts the day after dispatch, not
the day the order is placed.

| Service | Zone A | Zone B | Zone C |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Standard | 3 working days | 5 working days | 9 working days |
| Tracked | 2 working days | 4 working days | 7 working days |
| Next Day | next working day | not available | not available |

Next Day is offered for Zone A only and only for orders confirmed before 13:00.
An order confirmed at 13:01 ships the following working day and arrives the day
after that.

## Dispatch cut-off

The warehouse closes picking at 13:00 on working days. We do not pick on
weekends or on public holidays at the warehouse location.

An order placed on Friday at 16:00 with Standard service to Zone A is picked on
Monday and arrives on Thursday.

## Shipping costs

Shipping is charged per order, not per item.

| Order value | Zone A | Zone B | Zone C |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| below 60 | 5.90 | 11.90 | 24.90 |
| 60 and above | free | 6.90 | 19.90 |

Next Day carries a flat surcharge of 12.00 on top of the Standard rate and is
never free, whatever the order value.

Amounts are in our account currency and include tax at the rate that applies to
the delivery address.

## Oversized items

Tents over 6 kg, sleeping mats in hard cases and pack frames travel as oversized
freight. Oversized freight adds two working days to every service and is not
available as Next Day.

The product page marks oversized items before checkout. If an order mixes
oversized and normal items, the whole order travels as oversized freight and
arrives together.

## When a parcel is late

A parcel counts as late once it is two working days past the transit time for
its service and zone. Before that point we cannot open a case with the carrier,
because the carrier does not accept one.

Once a parcel is late, contact us with the order number. We open a carrier
enquiry, which takes up to five working days to answer. If the enquiry confirms
the parcel is lost, we send a replacement at no charge or refund the order in
full, whichever you prefer.

Replacements for lost parcels travel with Tracked service even when the original
order was Standard.
